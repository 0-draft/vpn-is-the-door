## Ten questions about your own appliance

An answer that depends on one person's memory counts as missing.

<div class="table-scroll" tabindex="0">

| # | Question | A complete answer | Why it is on the list |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Who owns the appliance, and who patches it under the contract? | A named person, and a contract clause that makes the vendor pass on advisories and apply fixes | The Osaka committee found the responsibility split between the hospital and its vendors undefined (p.14). Handa's reseller never passed the vulnerability notices on (technical volume, printed p.15). |
| 2 | Which firmware version is running, and when was it installed? | The exact version string, checked against the vendor's PSIRT advisories | NPA: in 2025, 40 of the 71 victims who answered had an intrusion device with security patches missing. |
| 3 | When does vendor support end for this model and version? | A date, and a funded replacement before it | CISA, FBI and the UK NCSC: "Maintain an inventory of all edge devices and their respective support timelines." |
| 4 | Can the management interface be reached from the internet? | No. Only from a separate management network | CCCS: "network management interfaces (NMIs) should not be directly exposed to the Internet." |
| 5 | What second factor do VPN users and administrators use? | No password-only accounts; phishing-resistant MFA for administrators | 39.8% of Japanese organizations NPA surveyed use ID and password only. Arctic Wolf saw logins to accounts with one-time passwords enabled. |
| 6 | Which accounts are local to the box, and when were their passwords last set? | A list with dates; nothing carried over from an old unit | SonicWall traced its 2025 wave to "local user passwords" that "were carried over during the migration and not reset." |
| 7 | Where do the logs go, and how long are they kept? | Off the box to a SIEM, kept longer than a zero-day stays undisclosed | NPA: only 25 of 102 ransomware victims in 2025 kept all their logs; 18 answers named an expired retention period. |
| 8 | When was the appliance last checked for compromise, and with what? | A date, the tool, and a check that does not rely on the appliance alone | CISA: "Ivanti's internal and previous external ICT failed to detect compromise." |
| 9 | After the last KEV entry for this product, were credentials reset? | Yes, with a date, covering local accounts, the LDAP bind account, RADIUS secrets and users | Fortinet, on the 87,000 leaked devices: "if the passwords were not reset, they remain vulnerable." |
| 10 | Is there a plan to stop exposing network-level VPN access? | A dated plan that treats ZTNA gateways as edge software too | CCCS encourages organizations "to move from overreliance on boundary protections like edge devices". [CVE-2025-0282](https://nvd.nist.gov/vuln/detail/CVE-2025-0282) also affected Ivanti Neurons for ZTA gateways. |

</div>
