## How the cases were chosen

A case is here only if the organization or an official report named the way in. Incidents where the route is known only from press reports, security researchers or the attackers' own claims are left out, even when often cited as VPN cases. Credentials quoted in the reports are not reproduced.

<CaseCounts lang="en" />

The case pages follow the Japan Transport Safety Board's accident reports, which state that an investigation is meant to prevent the next accident and not to assign blame.
