# Search research: what people type, and titles that match

Compiled 2026-10-27. All autocomplete lists below were fetched on 2026-10-27 from `https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&hl=<ja|en>&q=<seed>` and are quoted in the order Google returned them.

Limits of this evidence, stated up front:

- Autocomplete shows what is typed often enough to be suggested, in Google's order. It gives no volumes. Nothing here is a search-volume number.
- The requests carried `hl=ja` or `hl=en` but no `gl=`. They were made from the research tool's network, not from a Japanese residential IP, so the lists can differ slightly from what a user in Japan sees.
- Bing autosuggest (`api.bing.com/osjson.aspx`) was blocked by the sandbox and not read.
- Google Trends `explore` returned HTTP 429 and was not read. Only the Japan daily-trending RSS (`https://trends.google.com/trending/rss?geo=JP`) was readable.
- Ranking pages were read through web search results (titles and URLs), not by scraping Google's result page.

### 1.3 What is being searched now (October 2026)

- Japan daily-trending RSS, 2026-10-26: 「スマートex」 at 10000+ searches, driven by the 2026-10-26 announcement that about 170,000 エクスプレス予約／スマートEX member records leaked through unauthorized access at the mail-delivery contractor ユミルリンク, which itself reported a possible 5,162万件 leak ([Yahoo!ニュース／朝日新聞](https://news.yahoo.co.jp/articles/b7bca3752d568962d5fd9f47a56cbd8d0ce47b19), [新潟日報](https://www.niigata-nippo.co.jp/articles/-/931474)). The entry method had not been published in what was found. The site has no page on it and should not claim one; it shows that 「情報漏洩」「委託先 情報漏洩」 searches are active this month.
- Autocomplete carries month-stamped queries: 「情報漏洩 2026 年 10 月」「サイバー攻撃 2026年10月」「情報漏洩 2026年9月」.
- Asahi and ASKUL are still the most-completed incident names a year on: 「ランサムウェア」 alone completes to 「ランサムウェア アサヒ」, and 「感染経路」 completes to both. People pair them (「アサヒ アスクル 原因」), so a page that names both in its title matches a real query.
- 2026 cases on the site (穴吹興産, YCC情報システム, 精電舎電子工業, 両毛システムズ) are searched as company name + 「サイバー攻撃」「不正アクセス」「情報漏洩」, not + 「ランサムウェア」. Titles for those pages should carry the word the public uses next to the name.
- Older cases (半田病院, 大阪急性期, 名古屋港, 岡山県精神科医療センター) are searched with 「ランサムウェア」「報告書」「原因」. 「半田病院」 collides with 愛知の半田市立半田病院 (「半田病院 愛知」 is a suggestion), so the page should say 徳島 or つるぎ町立 in the title.

### 1.4 How people phrase it

- 原因 vs 侵入経路 vs 感染経路: all three complete. 「原因」 attaches to company names (アサヒ／アスクル ランサムウェア 原因). 「感染経路」 is the NPA's own word and completes with 割合／統計／ランキング／警察庁／最も多い. 「侵入経路」 completes with vpn／警察庁／メール. Use 感染経路 on the statistics page (NPA wording) and 侵入経路 on case pages; put the other word in the description.
- Asahi is searched as 「アサヒ vpn ランサムウェア」 and 「vpn 廃止 アサヒ」. Asahi itself says only 「ネットワーク機器」; abolishing remote-access VPN is in its prevention plan. The page can meet the VPN query in its body by stating both facts, but the title must say ネットワーク機器.
- 「二重脅迫」 is the completed term; the glossary uses 「二重恐喝」. Adding 「二重脅迫」 as an alternate label inside that glossary entry would match the query without changing the site's term.
- English searchers use "initial access vectors", "how does ransomware get into a network", product + "vulnerability" + year, and incident + "attack" ("asahi ransomware attack", "colonial pipeline ransomware attack", "boeing ransomware attack 2023").

What wins: company name first, then ランサムウェア, then 侵入経路／原因 and the concrete answer (「ネットワーク機器」「業務委託先用アカウント」). The news titles state the finding; the blog titles add question marks and bracketed months. This site should copy the first pattern and skip the second. At least one ranking explainer gives the 2025 VPN share as 62%, while the NPA workbook gives 61 of 92 (66.3% calc) (research/stats.md §1.1); the site's exact counts with sheet references are the difference it can show.

English results for Asahi lead with "Asahi ransomware attack" and the record count ([The Record](https://therecord.media/asahi-says-ransomware-incident-exposed-data), [TechRepublic](https://www.techrepublic.com/article/news-asahi-cyberattack-investigation/)); for initial access, insurer reports dominate ([At-Bay](https://www.at-bay.com/press_releases/2026-insursec-report-ransomware-vpn-sonicwall-attacks/), [Coalition](https://www.coalitioninc.com/announcements/cyber-threat-index-2025)). A government victim-survey figure for Japan is not among them, which is the gap the English statistics page can fill.

## 2. Proposed titles and descriptions

Length rules applied: JA title display width 64 or less (about 32 全角; half-width counts 1), JA description 120 characters or less, EN title 60 characters or less, EN description 155 characters or less. Every number is from the page it describes (frontmatter, `src/data/cases.json`, or chapter text).

Implementation note: `src/layouts/Base.astro` builds `${title} · ${siteTitle}` for every page, and `Chapter.astro` uses the same `title` as the H1. The strings below are the full title element. Adding a `seoTitle` field (frontmatter for chapters, `cases.json` for cases) that Base uses verbatim, without the automatic suffix, keeps H1 and title independent. The suffix 「｜扉はVPNだった」 adds 15 width units and " · The door was the VPN" adds 23 characters; it is included only where the total still fits.

### 4.1 JSON-LD

Every page except 404:

- `BreadcrumbList`: `itemListElement` of `ListItem` with `position`, `name`, `item` (absolute URL). Home → Cases → Asahi; Home → For engineers → Evidence. Names in the page language.

Home (`/`, `/ja/`):

- `WebSite`: `name` (「扉はVPNだった」 / "The door was the VPN"), `alternateName` (the other-language name), `url`, `inLanguage` (`ja` or `en`).

Chapters and case pages:

- `Article`. Google lists no required fields; recommended are `headline`, `image`, `datePublished`, `dateModified`, `author` with `name` and `url` ([Google](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/article)).
  - `headline`: the H1.
  - `description`: the meta description.
  - `inLanguage`: `ja` or `en`.
  - `image`: the existing OG PNG URL.
  - `datePublished`: date of the first revision in `src/data/revisions.ts`.
  - `dateModified`: date of the newest revision that is site-wide or lists the page in `pages`. Same source as the page history, so the markup cannot drift from what the page shows.
  - `author`: `Person` with `name` and `url` as the owner wants to be credited. Leave out if the owner prefers no named author.
  - `isPartOf`: the `WebSite` by `@id`.
  - `citation`: the primary-source URLs the page cites (for case pages, the URLs behind `vectorSource` and `sources`).
  - Case pages: `about` as `Organization` with `name` from `org` and, optionally, `sameAs` to the organization's official notice page. Do not mark up the incident as `Event`; it adds nothing Google uses and invites date errors.

`/where-they-came-in/` (both languages), in addition:

- `Dataset`. Google requires `name` and a 50–5000 character `description`; `distribution.contentUrl` is required only if a download is offered ([Google](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/dataset)).
  - `name`: "Ransomware infection routes reported to Japan's National Police Agency, 2021 to 2026 H1" / 「警察庁に報告されたランサムウェア被害の感染経路（2021年〜2026年上半期）」.

### 4.2 Sitemap

- The live `sitemap-0.xml` has 54 URLs (27 pages in two languages) with `xhtml:link` en/ja alternates and no `lastmod` (read 2026-10-27).
- Google ignores `changefreq` and `priority` and uses `lastmod` only when it is accurate to the last significant change ([Google](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/sitemaps/build-sitemap)).
- `@astrojs/sitemap` sets per-URL `lastmod` through `serialize(item)`, where `item.lastmod` is an ISO date string ([Astro docs](https://docs.astro.build/en/guides/integrations-guide/sitemap/)). Compute it from `REVISIONS` exactly like `dateModified`: newest revision that is site-wide or lists the locale-free path. One source for page history, JSON-LD and sitemap.

### 4.3 hreflang, canonical, robots

- hreflang en/ja/x-default and self-canonical are already emitted in `Base.astro`; the 404 page is `noindex` with no alternates. Nothing to change.
- `https://0-draft.github.io/robots.txt` returns 404 (2026-10-27), which crawlers treat as no restrictions. A project site under `/vpn-is-the-door/` cannot serve robots.txt for the host; only a `0-draft/0-draft.github.io` repository could. If the owner wants a `Sitemap:` line there, that is the place; otherwise submitting the sitemap in Search Console is enough.

- Verify with the HTML-tag method (a `google-site-verification` meta in `Base.astro`) or an HTML file in `public/`. Both need the token from the owner's Google account.
